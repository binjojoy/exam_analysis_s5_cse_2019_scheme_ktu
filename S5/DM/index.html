Tactical Study Strategy

✅ Strategy for Passing (Focus on 30-40 Marks)

  • **Master the Frameworks (M5):** The Sendai Framework is mandatory.
  • **Core Concepts (M2):** Learn the Qualitative vs. Quantitative Risk Assessment methods.
  • **Cycle Basics (M3):** Understand the DRM Cycle and the difference between Prevention and Mitigation.
  • **Part A (Quick Wins):** Memorize all differentiations (Risk/Crisis, Hazard/Disaster, Prevention/Mitigation) and list six relief organizations.

⭐ Strategy for High Scores (Beyond the Basics)

  • **Detailed Explanations:** Focus on writing 10-12 points for major topics (e.g., benefits of Stakeholder Participation, factors determining Response).
  • **Institutional Hierarchy (M5):** Know the roles of NDMA, SDMA, and NIDM in detail, as they often rotate.
  • **Rotational Picks (M1):** Prepare the structure and composition of the Lithosphere/Biosphere.
  • **Diagrams:** Use diagrams for the DRM Cycle and Earth's structure wherever possible to boost marks.
